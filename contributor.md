# 🤝 Contributing to Heritage Study

First of all, thank you for wanting to make **Heritage Study** better! Heritage Study is a community-driven, open-source web application designed to help students access high-quality, structured academic resources.

Every single contribution—no matter how small—helps a classmate prepare better for their next exam.

---

## 📚 What Can You Contribute?

You can contribute **anything** that makes the website fuller, more comprehensive, and highly functional! Here are some key areas where we need your help:

### 1. Study Materials
*   **Handwritten & Typed Notes:** High-quality scans or typed summaries of class lectures.
*   **Syllabus & E-Books:** Official curriculum guides and helpful reference textbooks.
*   **Past Question Papers:** Mid-semester and end-semester university/college question papers.
*   **Solutions & Answer Keys:** Step-by-step solved papers for previous years' exams.
*   **Subject Guides:** "Last-night preparation" checklists and high-priority study topics.

### 2. Interactive Mind Maps
*   **Visual Summaries:** Flowcharts, diagrams, and structural maps of complex chapters.
*   **Quick-Revision Sheets:** One-page cheat sheets summarizing formulas, key definitions, or algorithm workflows.

### 3. Practice Quizzes & Questions
*   **MCQs:** Multiple-choice questions for any module.
*   **Quiz Sets:** Structured quiz datasets with detailed step-by-step explanations for correct/incorrect choices.
*   **Exam Sprint Materials:** Core suggestions and structured high-impact items for our **Exam Sprint** module.

### 4. Technical Improvements & Bug Fixes
*   **UI/UX Improvements:** Enhance responsiveness, layout aesthetics, or add delightful micro-interactions.
*   **Feature Requests:** Help us implement better search, bookmarking, PDF viewing, or progress tracking.
*   **Performance & Bug Patches:** Optimizing bundle size, fixing styling alignment issues, or updating database schemas.

---

## 🛠️ How Can You Contribute?

We want to make the contribution process as easy as possible. You don't have to be a coder to contribute!

### Method A: For Content Creators (Non-Technical)
If you have study materials, notes, or mind maps:
1.  **Email Us:** Direct your files to [arghyajana8348@gmail.com](mailto:arghyajana8348@gmail.com) with the subject line: `[Contribution] Subject Name - Note Type`.
2.  **Include Details:** Please specify:
    *   **Subject Name** (e.g., Computer Organization & Architecture)
    *   **Module Number / Topic** (e.g., Module 2: Memory Organization)
    *   **Your Name** (so we can credit you on the Contributors wall!)
    *   **Format:** PDFs, high-quality images, or markdown files are preferred.

### Method B: For Developers (Technical)
If you want to contribute to the codebase:
1.  **Fork the Repository:** Head to [our GitHub repository](http://github.com/arghyajana8348-arch/heritage-study) and fork it.
2.  **Create a Branch:** Create a branch for your feature or bug fix (`git checkout -b feature/amazing-feature`).
3.  **Implement & Test:** Make your changes, ensure there are no linter/TypeScript compilation errors.
4.  **Submit a Pull Request (PR):** Open a PR back to our `main` branch. Provide a clear description of your changes and why they are valuable.

---

## 🏆 Contributor Guidelines & Code of Conduct

*   **Originality:** Please ensure you have the right to share the materials you upload. Avoid uploading strictly copyrighted commercial materials without authorization.
*   **Clarity:** Scan notes under good lighting using scanning apps (like Adobe Scan, vFlat, or CamScanner) to ensure high legibility.
*   **Respect:** Be encouraging, kind, and supportive of fellow students and contributors.

---

## ❤️ Rewards & Recognition

Every contributor gets their name on the **Contributors Wall** in the **About Us** page of the app. Your work will directly impact hundreds of students preparing for their exams.

Let's make study prep easy and collaborative for everyone! 🚀
